Logo

Use the primary two-color logo wherever its orange holds WCAG AA against the background. Switch to all-black or all-white where it doesn't. Minimum width is 100px on screen.

Clear space

  • Keep clear space equal to the letter height of the logotype on every side.
  • Don't let it crowd the edge of a section or sit tight against other graphics.
  • Don't recolor, rotate, stretch, add shadows or effects, or scale parts independently.
  • Don't place it on busy backgrounds.

Neutrals

Color rules

  • Black for all headline and body copy on light and primary-palette backgrounds.
  • White for headline and body copy on maroon, dark green and dark blue.
  • Orange may emphasize words in a headline on beige or light beige. On orange backgrounds, emphasize with white.
  • White buttons on orange backgrounds.
  • No white or primary-palette color (orange, amber, yellow) as headline or body text.
  • No red, teal or blue as backgrounds, and no secondary colors for headlines or body text.
  • No text pairing that fails WCAG AA.

Approved pairings

Contrast ratios calculated from the brand hex values. AA works for all text. AA large only works at 24px+, or 18.66px+ bold, and for icons and graphics. Fail is not for text.

Brand shapes

Rounded squares and rectangles on an even grid. Use them in collections of two or more to bring color into a layout, or singly to hold a photo or icon.

Module 96px
Gap 20px
Radius 16px

Measured from the guide's vector art: radius ≈ 16% of the module, gap ≈ 20%. Scale all three together.

Shape rules

  • Any primary-palette color can fill a shape.
  • Red, teal or blue may appear sparingly as a single accent in a collection (teal above).
  • Keep spacing even and the corner radius identical across every shape, photo and card in a composition.
  • Don't use red, teal and blue together in one collection.
  • Don't rotate shapes or mix radii.

Iconography

Thin-line icons with rounded corners, in the style of flaticon.com's thin line set. One color per set. The icons below are placeholders drawn to that style.

Sizes

  • Black icons on orange, amber or yellow.
  • Orange icons on light or dark backgrounds. Surface classes set this automatically.
  • Don't mix icon colors within a set, layout or collection.

Photography

Natural light, neutral-to-warm white balance, real and candid. Three subjects: learning leaders (1–3 people, tech present), teams and spaces (4+ people, open modern spaces), and consulting and learning experiences (welcoming body language, business settings, not higher-ed).

Web adaptations

The guide is written for print and slides. These are the decisions made to carry it to the browser, worth confirming with the brand owner.

DecisionWhy
Added deep purple for hover, secondary button text and linksBrand purple is 4.05:1 on beige and 4.34:1 on light beige, just under AA for small text. Deep purple passes on every light surface.
Charcoal #3A3F3E for secondary text, not dark grayDark gray is 3.99:1 on white. Keep it to borders, large text and disabled states.
Orange and white emphasis limited to large textOrange on beige is 3.06:1 and white on orange 3.49:1, which pass only as large text. The guide uses both in headlines, so this holds the intent.
White buttons on orange use black textThe guide shows white buttons on orange but not their label color; black gives 21:1.
Responsive type via clamp()72px headlines don't fit phones. Desktop sizes are exactly as specified.
4px spacing scale and 68-character line lengthNot in the guide; gives the report consistent rhythm and readable paragraphs.
One radius (16px) for shapes, cards and photos; pills for buttonsThe guide forbids mixing radii within a composition.
Light theme onlyThe brand has no dark mode. Dark sections use maroon, dark green or dark blue surfaces instead.
Focus ring: 2px deep purple, 3px offsetKeyboard accessibility; not covered by the guide.
